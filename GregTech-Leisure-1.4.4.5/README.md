
# GregTech Leisure

## 格雷科技休闲版

A low-difficulty, multi-progress GregTech modpack centered on [GregTech CEU Modern](https://github.com/GregTechCEu/GregTech-Modern).

## Additions & Modifications

* Added powerful steam multiblock structures, bringing steam-age productivity to the level of IV-tier large machines.
* Increased steam output and power generation, and added new methods of energy production, making energy less of a problem.
* Assembly warehouses and multi-energy storage are available earlier (LV), simplifying early circuit production, wireless networks, and providing various powerful tools to reduce early-game burden.
* Each planet has bedrock fluids and a complete void resource generation system, solving resource issues.
* GTLCore includes configuration options, allowing players to adjust game difficulty to their preference.

## Main Progression

* **Steam:** Collect basic resources, build larger and stronger steam machines, steelmaking.
* **Low Voltage (LV):** Basic electric machines, electric blast furnace, slaughterhouse, wireless network, AE startup.
* **Medium Voltage (MV):** Silicon crystals, oil processing, universal factory, upgraded machines.
* **High Voltage (HV):** Processors, large fission reactor, first-tier rocket moon landing, distillation tower, titanium processing, void miner.
* **Ultra-High Voltage (UV):** Nano processors, 2nd–4th tier rockets, tungsten and platinum processing.
* **Extreme Conductive Voltage:** Quantum processors, 5th-tier rockets, alloy smelters, fission reactors, large machines, assembly lines.
* **Drastic Voltage:** Crystal processors, silicon rock processing, 6th-tier rockets, Fusion MK1, computers, research stations.
* **Zero Voltage:** Wet component processors, Fusion MK2, 7th-tier rockets, integrated ore processing, integrated petrochemicals, nano forging furnace, chemical distorting machine.
* **Limit Voltage:** Bioactive processors, Fusion MK3, circuit assembly lines, space elevator, super smelters.
* **Extreme-High Voltage:** Optical processors, precision photolithography, stellar forge, supercharged silicon rock bombs, laser-version giant machines.
* **Extreme-Super Voltage:** Exotic processors, supercritical synthesis, bedrock, refined gold processing, supercomputers, second-tier nano forging furnace.
* **Extreme-Giga Voltage:** Universal processors, mass generator, quark processing, UU matter furnace, extra-dimensional plasma furnace.
* **Extreme-Top Voltage:** Super-causal processors, replicators, dragon processing, third-tier nano forging furnace, quantum instruments, infinity.
* **Overload Voltage:** Spacetime circuits, universe simulation, spacetime metals, eternal processing, gate of creation.
* **Upper Limit Voltage:** Command block processing, creative item synthesis.
